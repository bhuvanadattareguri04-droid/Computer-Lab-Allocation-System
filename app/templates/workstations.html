Add Workstations

Add multiple computers in one operation. Sequential IDs are generated automatically (e.g. PC-06, PC-07…). Newly added computers are immediately assigned to waiting students in FIFO order.

Workstation IDs are generated sequentially. The semaphore permit count is updated automatically. If students are in the FIFO waiting queue, newly added computers are assigned to them immediately.

Remove Workstation

Only available (unoccupied) workstations can be removed. Release any active student session before removing a workstation.

Removing a workstation releases its semaphore permit and deletes the record from SQLite. Occupied workstations cannot be removed until the student logs out.

Release Workstation / Logout

Release an occupied workstation session. When released, the workstation is automatically reassigned to the first student in the FIFO waiting queue.

Workstation sessions can also be released directly by clicking "Log Out / Release" on any occupied card below.
